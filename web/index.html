After a guardian recovery: sync the owner validator

Agent Bank owner operations are checked by an OwnableValidator with its own owner list. A guardian recovery changes only the Safe's owners: this makes the validator list exactly the Safe's owners again (the old key loses its access), signed by your new owner wallet. It moves no funds.

Plans appear here. Nothing is signed or sent until you press the send button and approve each request in your wallet.

@agentbank/exit . Open this file over http(s) (for example python3 -m http.server in its folder): browser wallets do not inject into file:// pages. The command-line tool (agentbank-exit) does the same with an RPC URL and your owner key or a hardware wallet.
